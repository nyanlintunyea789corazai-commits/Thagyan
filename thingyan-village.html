    Thingyan Village

    သင်္ကြန်ရွာ

    It is the water festival. Walk the village, splash your friends, spray from the mandat stages, cook mont lone yay paw, release fish for merit, and wash the elders' hair to welcome the new year.

    Phone: drag anywhere to walk, tap the round button to act.
    Computer: arrow keys or WASD to walk, Space to act.
